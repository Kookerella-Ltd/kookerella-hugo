# Kookerella

Kookerella is a specialist software development consultancy founded to deliver system integrations, tooling and reporting development for media organisations in the broadcast and VOD sector.

Our focus is on simple, reliable solutions where the focus is on delivering value, not delivering technology for technology's sake, but we have over the years developed particular areas of specialist knowledge.

We are not wedded to any particular technology or technique but much of our tooling is written in F#, some in C#, and some in Scala. We have extensive experience of delivering XSLT.

This website offers some insight and hopefully help and advice to others struggling to solve the same issues as us — see our [products](/products/), read the [blog](/posts/), or browse our [open-source work on GitHub](https://github.com/kookerella-ltd). Feel free to [get in touch](mailto:admin@kookerella.com) for a general chat or a specific question.
